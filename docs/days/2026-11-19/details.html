Method & limits

Virality V = 0.35·Reach + 0.25·Velocity + 0.20·Engagement + 0.20·Spread, computed as within-platform percentiles (raw counts are not comparable across platforms). A story qualifies at V ≥ 60, via a ≥3-platform cluster, or as a top-10 platform item with top-quintile engagement. Tiers: Mega ≥ 85 · Viral 70–84 · Rising 60–69 · Watch = qualified by cluster/top-10 rule only.

US only. Sources are the US editions/charts of each platform. Reddit publishes no US-only feed — r/popular from US egress is US-weighted, not US-exclusive.

Media. If a source provides no usable image, the card falls back to the source's video, then to a designed title tile. Broken image placeholders are never shown. Where an item's own page provides no image, the card may use the top relevant web-search image for the topic, credited to its source. Images are shown at their native aspect ratio, never stretched; portraits sit on a plain matte.

Links. Links prefer a destination you can open without an account: where a story runs on several platforms, the card links the login-free version; X-only topics link to a news search, with the X link kept here and labeled "login required".

Facebook / Instagram. No public anonymous trending surface exists since Meta shut down CrowdTangle in Aug 2024 (the Content Library is application-only). Reported as BLOCKED rather than filled with proxied or invented data.

LinkedIn. No public trending feed; only LinkedIn News editor picks, no metrics — labeled LIMITED, and a small source cannot mint a high tier on its own.

Summaries and "why" lines are generated from the sources' own text and measured metrics only.
